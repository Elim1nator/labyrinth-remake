- The Ice Wizard, boss of every Icy Prison floor: small ice orbs and big orbs that burst into blue explosions, blizzards that cut the view to 3 tiles for 5-10 s, and a shield bubble at 75/50/25% health held up by 2-5 flying ice spikes that come at you from behind.
- He drops the Frozen Wand: balloons freeze (+1 damage), bounce off what they hit and roll about on the floor before vanishing.
- No more file-size cap in the tests (keep it small; the size is still reported).
