# M1.74 brief

Two new run items, both from Jacob.

**Snowball.** A rare Snowman drop (about 7% per kill, at most one per run) that turns thrown balloons into snowballs: Jacob's snowball sprite and held-snowball hand, a 1 second slow on hit, and a white puff (with a puff sound) instead of the water splash. Balloon colour stacking and damage stay on; the Frozen Wand's +1 and bounce still apply (bounce still skips the puff). CONTINUE keeps it. Old saves without the field still load.

**Ferryman's Coin.** Old Vaults only. A Debt Collector has a 30% chance to drop it. Every Old Vaults shop always stocks it. Max one per run: once you own it, collectors stop dropping it and the shop lists it as sold. While you hold it, opening a locked door or a coin door gives +3 coins. The Music Box pays +3 for each locked or coin door it opens; fences do not. A coin door restored by CONTINUE does not pay out again. CONTINUE keeps it. Old saves without the field still load.
