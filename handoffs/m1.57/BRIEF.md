# M1.57 brief

After the Bather shipped on Grok-Code-4 (unreleased M1.56), main landed a different M1.56 (no Infernal riders). This release is therefore M1.57 on Grok-Code-5, and it ports the Baths work plus Jacob's follow-ups:

- Replace the Bather's art with the supplied 4-frame marble statue sheet. Puddles become pool-water blue. Confirm it only spawns on Tiled Baths floors.
- Keep the old 5-frame hunched art as a new mob, **the Slick Man**, that only spawns on Slate Halls: high HP, 30% projectile reflect (can hurt the player), fog forced to 5 blocks while nearby, heavy wet-stone voice and footsteps.
- Keep the Plunger (Baths-only loot, one use, one per floor, uncommon).
