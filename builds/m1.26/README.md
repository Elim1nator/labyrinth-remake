# Kersplat Labyrinth: The Descent — M1.26

Release build (no debug hook unless `?debug`); QA build in `../m1.26-qa/`.

## What changed since M1.25

- The Screamer King replaces King Gloop (floors 5, 20, 35, ...): a crowned purple Screamer that flies, spits a huge purple orb or a fan of small ones, and calls plain Screamers as minions.
- Boss floors: the barred portal vanishes after the boss intro; the open portal appears when the boss dies (a few cells away if you're standing on its spot).

Frozen build; sha256 in `../SHA256SUMS`.
