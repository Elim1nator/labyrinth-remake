# M1.75 brief: the Clockwork Mourner and the Tin Heart

The owner's request, with the art supplied (a rusted tin heart and a 3-frame wind-up robot):

- Both only appear on the **Rust Works** floors.
- **Tin Heart** (item): makes you immune to Rust and speeds up your throws.
- **The Clockwork Mourner** (mob, 7 HP, tanky): a wind-up figure in a rusted mask whose every bite Rusts you. A common mob on the Rust Works floors. When it dies it shatters with gear particles.
- The robot has 3 frames: right foot, standing, left foot. Use them for its walk and its stand. It makes a clockwork sound.
