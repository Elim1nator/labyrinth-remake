# Kersplat Labyrinth: The Descent — M1.17.1

Release build (no debug hook unless `?debug`); QA build in `../m1.17.1-qa/`.

## What changed since M1.17

- **Ambience you can hear:** the drone was below 60 Hz, which small speakers can't play. It now sits at about 165–235 Hz with a brighter filter. The distant drips, chimes, creaks, wind, whispers and bells are louder and come every 3–9 s. It is still quiet: measured about −37 dB on floor 1.
- **Treasure Knight HP:** 10 on floor 2 (his first floor), +3 per floor: 34 on floor 10, 64 on floor 20.

Frozen build; sha256 in `../SHA256SUMS`.
