# M1.62 review
- Music Box only drops on Timber Attic floors (loot, shop, slot and every other source). Using it plays an eerie music-box tune for the rest of the level and permanently opens every door, including locked and coin doors and the hidden cracked wall, then is used up.
- The Rafter Doll only spawns on Timber Attic floors, with at least 1 on L2–4 and 2 from L5, and is skipped by the L3 kettle-swap and the Rattlebones swap. It has no ranged elite.
- It is drawn at half a normal mob's size with a matching hitbox, is fast, and after a bite it flees then comes back. The bite applies Rust for 15 s: throws are slower and have short range. The RUSTED pill must stay clear of the touch buttons.
- Art: 2 feet-centred, bottom-aligned walk frames with a transparent background.
