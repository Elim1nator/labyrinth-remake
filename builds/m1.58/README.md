# Kersplat Labyrinth: The Descent — M1.58

Release build (no debug hook unless `?debug`); QA build in `../m1.58-qa/`.

## What changed since M1.57

- Theme featured mobs keep a guaranteed minimum after later generation steps: at least 1 on that theme, 2 from floor 5 (the Bather, the Lichen Face, the Slick Man, and the Mortared Man).
- The Bather's statue walk is feet-centred and bottom-aligned (no wobble) and twice as fast.
- Treasure Knights are unchanged: one per floor from 2, with an occasional second from a coin door.

Frozen build; sha256 in `../SHA256SUMS`.
