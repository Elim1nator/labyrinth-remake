# M1.56 brief

Theme: **Tiled Baths** (the existing early theme).

- Item: **Plunger**. Lets you fall to the next floor once via a black hole and is then used up.
- Mob: **The Bather** (4 HP), a hunched shape trailing a wet trail that leaves slick damaging puddles behind it.

The Bather has 4 base HP, scales like other mobs, and is a slow hunched ground mob that chases the player. Its puddles last a few seconds, deal light damage and sap traction (an ice-like slide). Flyers skip them; Rubber Boots ignore them like other goo. It has a wet, sloshing procedural voice and dripping footsteps.

The Plunger is a use-item in the normal loot and shop pools. Using it opens a black hole at your feet and drops you to the next floor (same as taking the exit: run state is kept). It is then used up. On a sealed boss floor, or on the last floor, it is blocked with a clear message and is not consumed.
